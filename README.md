## Overview

**TTTpulse** is the advanced Sentiment & Social Intelligence skill in the Thinking Trade Tech suite.  
It measures the real pulse of any Bankr token by combining on-chain behavior, fee momentum, volume quality, holder dynamics, and narrative strength into a clear, actionable **Sentiment Score (0–100)**.

While TTTsignal focuses on technical direction and TTTstrat builds executable strategies, TTTpulse answers the critical question:

> “What is the actual mood and narrative power behind this token right now?”

Built exclusively for the Bankr ecosystem on **Base** and **Robinhood Chain**.

> Powered by data. Driven by intelligence.

## How It Works

1. Fetches official Bankr fee data (`dailyEarnings`) + market data  
2. Analyzes fee momentum, volume quality, holder health, and price-fee alignment  
3. Calculates a transparent **Sentiment Score (0–100)**  
4. Determines Narrative Strength  
5. Delivers clear drivers + actionable insight  
6. Offers seamless handoff to TTTsignal / TTTrisk / TTTstrat  

All analysis is fully data-driven and transparent.
